MAKE YOURSELF AT HOME

A little orientation.

Explore the model
Choose Edit furniture to move chairs and tables in a clean view. Press Space while dragging, or double-tap on touch screens, to rotate by 90°. Drag elsewhere to orbit. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Right-drag to pan. Drops outside the apartment or across a wall return to the previous position. Furniture positions and rotations are saved in this browser. Reset view restores the original furniture layout and camera.
Walk inside
Use W A S D or arrow keys to move. Drag the scene to look around, or click to lock the mouse. Press Esc to release it. On touch screens, drag to look and use the arrow pad to move.
Find your space
Select any room to step straight inside. Switch to 3D Overview to see the whole home again.
Set the atmosphere
Switch between daylight and evening, or raise the walls in the overview.

The supplied plan defines the layout. Materials, heights, and unmarked dimensions are interpreted, not construction measurements.
